About TimePie

Empowering creators with beautiful time tracking that actually works

Our Mission

At TimePie, we believe that time tracking shouldn't be a chore. We're on a mission to transform how creators, freelancers, and teams manage their time by providing tools that are not only powerful but also beautiful and intuitive to use.

Founded in 2023 by a team of passionate creators and developers, TimePie was born from our own frustration with existing time tracking solutions. We wanted something that would make us excited to track our time, not dread it.

Today, we're proud to serve thousands of creators worldwide, helping them gain insights into their productivity, create stunning reports for clients, and ultimately build more successful businesses.
